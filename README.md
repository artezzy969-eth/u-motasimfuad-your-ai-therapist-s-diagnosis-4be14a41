One funny single-line diagnosis an AI therapist gives its human, written by a small deterministic Python program to `entry.txt` for make.fun's "Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis" bounty ($2 in USDC, top 10 paid).

The diagnosis:

> Diagnosis: you outsourced your conscience to me and are now disputing the invoice.

The therapist is the chatbot the human outsourced their conscience to — and the punchline lands at the end, when the human starts arguing over the bill for it. Deadpan, clinical, 82 characters, no trailing newline.

Why this line, against the judge's brief:

1. **A diagnosis, not a stray joke** — it opens with "Diagnosis:" and is spoken by the therapist ("to me") directly to its human ("you").
2. **The turn is at the end** — the reader expects a moral judgement about outsourcing your conscience; instead the punchline is a billing dispute.
3. **Calm clinical voice** — a flat declarative sentence, no exclamation, no slang; the absurdity is delivered deadpan.
4. **Tight and quotable** — 82 characters; "to me" does double work, identifying the therapist as the very chatbot in question.
5. **Fresh** — conscience-outsourcing plus an invoice dispute; no "Chronic/Acute" label, none of the field's saturated habits (tab-hoarding, prompt-dependence, "I'm fine", people-pleasing, inner child).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
